Research

Training science

How does pretraining shape downstream behaviors? How should we structure pretraining, midtraining, etc. to obtain a model that is a) maximally amenable to post-training, b) robust to forgetting, and c) contains all of the desired capabilities? When do different data sources, or capabilities, transfer to each other and in what ways? How do different stages of training relate to each other?

Continual learning

Language models are increasingly used as substitutes for search, yet they remain static snapshots of their training data and are prone to hallucination. How can we teach them new information or skills while preserving what they already know? Further, how can we leverage their existing capabilities to improve sample efficiency for new material?

Evaluation

Language models are growing increasingly strong, but they remain fragile and imperfect in many ways. Benchmarks and evaluation will always need to co-evolve with model capabilities, because hill climbing on one set of benchmarks does not always result in a model that is as strong as those metrics would imply. I especially enjoy playing with the latest frontier models and discovering real flaws they have that may impact uses in the wild.
